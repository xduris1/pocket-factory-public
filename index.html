Pocket Factory is an Android factory-building game provided under the Questionable Engineering developer name. This policy describes the current Android beta, including optional Google AdMob rewarded ads and Google Play purchases.

Local game data

The game saves your factory layout, production, research, shipping progress, settings and boost wallet on your device. The wallet includes a randomly generated factory identifier, purchased time, machine reward timers, hashed purchase references and random reward references used to prevent duplicate grants. This beta does not upload your factory saves to a developer-operated cloud account.

Local data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android's storage behaviour. Clearing storage can remove your consumable boost bank and game progress.

Google Play purchases

Optional factory-time purchases are processed by Google Play. The game uses product information, purchase status and purchase tokens to deliver and consume a purchased pack. Its local wallet keeps hashed references for recovery and duplicate prevention. The game does not store payment-card numbers. Google may make transaction records available to the developer through its merchant services.

Google handles its payment and account data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional rewarded advertising and consent

Viewing a rewarded ad is optional; ordinary game progression remains available without watching ads. An eligible completed rewarded ad grants a temporary boost to the selected machine.

Google AdMob and its consent SDK process data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. According to Google's SDK disclosure, this can include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID. The data processed depends on device settings, consent, the ad configuration and Google's services.

Where required, a Google consent message provides privacy choices before ad requests. You can revisit required choices through the game's Ad privacy control. You can also use Android's advertising privacy settings. Refusing consent does not disable ordinary factory gameplay; ad availability depends on Google's consent rules.

Google processes and retains its service data under its own policies and may process it outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure.

Support and your choices

If you contact the developer, information you voluntarily send, such as your email address, issue description or diagnostic files, is used to respond and troubleshoot. Support information is retained only as necessary for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Do not include passwords or payment-card information in support requests.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact the developer for information it controls; use Google's privacy controls for information Google controls. We cannot retrieve a local factory save that you have not provided to us.

Data handling and security

Local game files use Android's app-specific storage controls. The boost wallet stores hashed purchase references rather than payment-card details. Google handles advertising, consent and purchase communications through its SDKs and services under its documented security practices. Keep your device and any copies of your saves secure.
